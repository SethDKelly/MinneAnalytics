## 1. Interpretation

This matrix is a discovery trace, not a concept mapping.

- `IL-*` references come from the 001-B historical intent ledger.
- `PR-*` references come from the 001-C problem inventory.
- `AN-*` references come from the 001-C actor-need inventory.
- `PU-*` references come from the 001-C purpose inventory.

A many-to-many mapping is expected.

No row means that the referenced purpose is a future concept, and no lack of one-to-one correspondence is a defect. The purpose of the trace is to make reasoning provenance visible before 001-D begins candidate concept discovery.
