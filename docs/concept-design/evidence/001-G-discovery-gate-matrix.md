## 1. Gate basis

001-G consolidates the evidence chain rather than rerunning discovery from implementation structure.

A candidate can enter formal specification only when the preceding phases establish:

1. a supported problem/actor need and focused purpose;
2. a plausible boundary independent of current schema/UI/API structure;
3. acceptable specificity, completeness, independence, and bounded genericity;
4. a concise concept-local operational principle;
5. no unresolved contradiction severe enough to make formal state/action design premature.

A candidate may be provisionally admitted when the boundary is coherent enough to specify but one explicit falsification condition must remain visible.

## 4. Superseded discovery shapes

The following earlier hypotheses are preserved in historical records but are not valid names/boundaries for Phase 002 unless new evidence reopens them:

- broad `Disclosure` → Controlled Disclosure;
- `Retraction` → Withdrawal;
- merged `Coverage` → Coverage Target + derived composition;
- broad `Communication` → Dispatch;
- generic `Obligation` → over-generalized and superseded;
- standalone Authorization, Export, Audit Trail → demoted;
- Registration → later demoted by operational-principle falsification.

## 5. Non-concepts/derived behavior reaffirmed

Phase 001 does not admit concepts for:

- `Conference`, `Program`, or `Submission` as broad coordinating aggregates;
- current role names or authentication mechanism;
- `ProgramStatus`, `AbstractReviewStatus`, `DeckStatus`, `Score`, or `Theme` as concept boundaries;
- effective participation;
- current/stale Evaluation;
- needs-score/rescore queues;
- actual composition or coverage gaps;
- edit eligibility;
- schedulable eligibility;
- publication eligibility;
- Dispatch recipient eligibility;
- integration-provider identity;
- CSV/heatmap/dashboard/route/API representations.
