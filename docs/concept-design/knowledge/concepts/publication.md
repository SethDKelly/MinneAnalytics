# Purpose

Intentionally expose eligible material to a public audience.

# Operational Principle

A publisher has material that may be made public. They intentionally publish an eligible item or collection, making that material available to the public audience. Material that is not intended for public exposure remains unavailable even if it exists and is otherwise ready. The publisher can later unpublish material without erasing the underlying material itself.

# Boundary

Publication owns publish/unpublish behavior and public availability. It does not establish [Deliverable](deliverable.md) readiness, organizer [Selection](selection.md), or internal retained historical closure in [Archive](archive.md).

Publication eligibility may later be composed from readiness, sharing intent, and application policy without duplicating those sources as Publication-owned state.

# Gate Decision

**Admitted to Phase 002 formal specification.** Public exposure remains independently meaningful from the historically overloaded “slide archive” terminology.
