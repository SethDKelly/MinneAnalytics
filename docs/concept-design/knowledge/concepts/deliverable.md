# Purpose

Obtain a required artifact and establish whether it is operationally ready.

# Operational Principle

A responsible party is expected to provide an artifact for a subject or commitment. The Deliverable records that requirement and accepts the provided artifact. A reviewer examines it and may record a concern while it is not ready. After the concern is resolved, the reviewer establishes that the Deliverable is ready for its intended downstream use.

# Boundary

Deliverable owns artifact requirement, provision, readiness review, concerns, and readiness state. It does not decide why the artifact became required, whether the underlying subject was [selected](selection.md), or whether a ready artifact should be publicly [published](publication.md).

# Gate Decision

**Admitted to Phase 002 formal specification.** The concept is coherent beyond the current presentation-deck instance and preserves readiness independently from public exposure.
