# Purpose

Deliver recipient-directed response about a subject, distinct from private judgment context.

# Operational Principle

A reviewer has a response about a subject that is intended for its originator rather than merely for the reviewer's private notes. The reviewer records the Feedback for that subject and intended recipient. The recipient can later inspect the response as information deliberately addressed to them, with its source and relevant context preserved.

# Boundary

Feedback owns intentional response content, its subject, source, intended recipient/audience, and relevant context. It does not create an [Evaluation](evaluation.md), require a [Revision](revision.md), or perform broad operational [Dispatch](dispatch.md).

Future attendee-to-organizer response remains a compatibility test, not part of the current proof of the concept boundary.

**Admitted to Phase 002 formal specification.** The current committee-to-originator case establishes a complete independent purpose.
