# Purpose

Establish a durable offered subject for organized consideration.

# Operational Principle

An originator creates a Proposal containing the subject they want others to consider and receives a stable reference to it. The Proposal remains the identifiable thing under consideration as later activity occurs around it, so participants return to the same Proposal rather than creating a new identity for every downstream action.

# Boundary

Proposal owns the durable identity/reference of the offered subject. It does not own change history, evaluation, organizer selection, participant withdrawal, classification, scheduling, deliverables, or publication.

Change-over-time belongs to [Revision](revision.md); organizer choice belongs to [Selection](selection.md); originator participation agency belongs to [Withdrawal](withdrawal.md).

**Admitted to Phase 002 formal specification.** The boundary survived purpose discovery, criteria review, and operational-principle falsification without depending on the current `Submission` aggregate.
