# Purpose

Establish a visible time-bounded opportunity during which a governed activity is ordinarily available.

# Operational Principle

An organizer defines that an opportunity opens at one time and closes at another. Before opening, participants can see that it is upcoming but cannot ordinarily act within it. During the interval it is open. After closing it is closed, while the window remains inspectable so participants can understand the timing that governed the opportunity. The organizer may explicitly adjust the interval when policy permits.

# Boundary

Availability Window owns the explicit interval and its upcoming/open/closed interpretation. It does not own the governed action, authorization, [Archive](archive.md), or exceptional override policy.

It may compose with actions such as creating a [Proposal](proposal.md) or recording a [Revision](revision.md) without needing those concepts' internal state.

# Gate Decision

**Provisionally admitted to Phase 002 formal specification.** Formal specification must prove that the concept remains a user-recognizable governed opportunity rather than generic calendar configuration or a pair of timestamps.
